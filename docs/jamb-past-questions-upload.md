# JAMB past question upload

Use the per-year `*_clean.csv` files from the supplied question bank. Do not upload a combined `1978_2026_clean.csv`, a cleaning log, or an extraction log as one paper. Select the year and subject explicitly in the admin portal; filenames are not trusted as metadata.

The [blank template](../public/templates/jamb-past-questions-template.csv) and [fictional sample](../public/templates/jamb-past-questions-sample.csv) show the accepted UTF-8 CSV layout. Headers must be exactly `Question`, `Option A` through `Option E`, `Correct Answer`, with optional `Passage`, `Explanation`, `Media URL`, and `Media Alt`. Option E may be blank. Provide at least two distinct, consecutive options. The answer starts with a letter A–E and may include ` — ` followed by the matching option text, as in the supplied files. Explanations and passages can span CSV lines when quoted. A media URL must use HTTPS and needs meaningful alternative text.

Each file is limited to 5 MB and 500 question rows. Validation reports every malformed row, missing answer, mismatched answer text, or duplicate question within the same paper. The admin must correct the source CSV and preview it again. Import creates an unpublished version; it never publishes automatically. Review and correct questions on the paper page, then publish. A newly published version retires the previous published version for the same year and subject. Existing student attempts retain their exact question snapshots and scores.

Apply the backend Prisma migration before using this page: run `npx prisma migrate deploy` from `bjot-backend` with its production `DATABASE_URL` and `DIRECT_URL` configured. Open `/jamb-past-questions` in the admin portal, select year and subject, upload a per-year CSV, choose **Validate & preview**, correct any listed rows, then **Import draft**. Review the draft and select **Publish paper**. Past papers are independent of the regular question bank; the selected subject is used only for its active title and the four-subject registration rule. The default paper configuration follows current CBT rules: at most 60 English questions, at most 40 for each other subject, 10 per subject for regular accounts, and two hours for a four-subject attempt. If a supplied year has fewer questions, its paper uses the available count. Student availability lists only published papers with usable questions.
